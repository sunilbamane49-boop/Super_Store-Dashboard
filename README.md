# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, regional trends, and product category performance.

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and KPI Cards. It enables users to monitor key business metrics and make data-driven decisions through an intuitive and interactive interface.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Standard Class Dominates Shipping

Standard Class contributes the highest share of sales, accounting for more than half of all shipments.

### 2. West Region Generates Strong Activity

The West region records the highest number of transactions, indicating strong market performance.

### 3. Consumer Segment Leads Demand

Consumer customers account for the largest quantity of orders compared to Corporate and Home Office segments.

### 4. Office Supplies Drive Most Transactions

Office Supplies is the most frequently purchased product category.

### 5. Top Cities Contribute Significant Revenue

Major cities such as New York and Los Angeles generate a substantial portion of total sales.

### 6. Seasonal Sales Trends Exist

Monthly order volume fluctuates throughout the year, with peak demand observed during certain months.
